The implementation patterns below reflect the work described in my résumé. Tradeoffs explain considerations relevant to these patterns; they are not a retrospective record of unreported client decisions.

01

Reusable orchestration

ENGINEERING CONSIDERATION

02

Context and throughput

Tune retrieval, chunk sizes, context management, semantic caching, and token controls.

ENGINEERING CONSIDERATION

03

Package services in Docker and deploy through ACR and AKS with health checks, replicas, and autoscaling.

ENGINEERING CONSIDERATION
