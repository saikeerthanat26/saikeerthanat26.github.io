01

Hybrid candidate retrieval

Combine BM25 lexical matching with embedding-based similarity and behavioral signals.

ENGINEERING CONSIDERATION

02

Scalable feature pipelines

ENGINEERING CONSIDERATION

ENGINEERING CONSIDERATION
